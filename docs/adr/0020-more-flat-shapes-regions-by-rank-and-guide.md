# ADR 0020: Flat shapes (galaxy, spider, web, honeycomb, solar system), regions by connection count, exclusive spots and a shape guide
- Status: accepted (user request 2026-10-16)
- Date: 2026-10-16

## Context
The user asked for flat shapes of a spiral galaxy, a spider, honey (a honeycomb) and a solar system with many notes as stars and the main notes as planets, each with decent space. Testing showed three gaps in the pack format: (1) a region could only pick notes by name, path or tag, not by how connected they are; (2) two planets chose the same target and overlapped (5 px apart); (3) line-art shapes (a web, a honeycomb) were invisible because only the notes are drawn.

## Choice
1. The search language gains `degree>=N` (also `>`, `<=`, `<`, `=`) and `top:N`. `top:N` is the N most connected of the notes being placed; the rank is worked out among the notes in the layout, so filters and the local graph are respected. It only has meaning where a rank is supplied (shape regions).
2. `points` shapes may have `regions` (each with `match` and `points`), and a region may be `exclusive`: its notes get one point each, most connected first, and are pulled straight to it (a per-note anchor in both Rust engines), not to the nearest point. The sun and the eight planets are exclusive regions of one point each, so no two ever overlap, and the more connected a planet, the closer to the sun.
3. A layout may set `guide: true` (and the user may switch on "Show the shape as a faint guide"): the shape's points are drawn as faint dots behind the graph in the flat view. Web, honeycomb and spider use it.
4. The shapes are generated by `bench/generate-shape-packs.mjs` as ordinary pack data (seeded, repeatable). Galaxy gains a flat layout from the same spiral.

## Consequences
Pack authors get rank-based regions, exclusive spots and a guide. The guide and anchors are flat/engine features that work in 3D engines too, but only the flat view draws the guide. Shapes made of thin lines still only approximately hold notes on the lines (repel pushes some into the gaps); the guide is what makes them readable.
